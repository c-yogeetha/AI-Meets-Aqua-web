# Known limitations

- The behavioural stress indicator is a project-defined, rule-based signal. It is not clinically validated and must not be used as a veterinary diagnosis.
- There is no validated goldfish detector benchmark from owner-labelled tank clips yet. The downloaded CC0 aquarium stand-in is not a goldfish-labelled benchmark.
- The application is scoped to goldfish, fixed-camera footage, approximately 1–6 fish, 10–90 second clips, at least 480p, and reasonably clear water. Conditions outside that envelope may lower reliability.
- Reflections, bubbles, surface shimmer, colour cast, exposure flicker, occlusion, decor colour, fish pattern/colour and small fish may reduce detector performance.
- ONNX is not eligible until separately evaluated on clip-level hold-out data. The classical detector is retained as the product default.
